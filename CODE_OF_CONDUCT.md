# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a friendly, respectful, and harassment-free project space for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Assume good intent, and ask clarifying questions when needed.
- Help newcomers understand the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block participants who make the project unsafe or unproductive.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately through the repository owner profile or website.
